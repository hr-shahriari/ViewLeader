About this example

The question: the whole loop — create a leader by clicking the model, then change every part of it.

  • Tools, one shot each; press the armed button again, or Escape, to cancel. New leader: click the model to place the arrow. Multi-point leader: click the arrow point, then each bend, and finish with Enter or a double-click. Rectangle, Revision cloud, Ink: drag across a wall.
  • Select: click a label; shift-, ⌘- or Ctrl-click toggles another in or out. Shift-drag empty space to rubber-band a marquee, or alt-drag to subtract — plain left-drag orbits here, because this page passes marquee: 'modifier' to keep the camera on the left button.
  • Edit by hand: drag a label to move it, drag an arrowhead grip onto something else, drag a route grip to bend a leg. Grips exist only on a selected leader, and the cursor changes over anything draggable.
  • Double-click a label to retitle it: Enter commits, Escape cancels.
  • The boxes report the selection, not the last value you picked: Leg, Leg routing and Style read the selected leader and grey out without one. Align needs two selected, Distribute needs three.
  • Keys: ⌘/Ctrl+Z undo · ⌘/Ctrl+Shift+Z redo · ⌘/Ctrl+A select all · Delete · Escape.
  • Save and Load round-trip the document through localStorage — replace swaps it into the live instance without reconstructing it.
  • Orbit until an anchor swings behind the building — that leg draws dashed and dimmed (ISO 128: dashed means hidden) while the label stays at full strength.
