About this example

The question: who owns the ViewLeader instance in a Vue app, and what happens when the boundary is replaced?

Try: Replace boundary changes the key on the rendered element. Vue disposes the prior instance with its effect scope, the composable constructs a new one for the new element, and the watcher seeds it again; the note’s title counts up (Vue #2, #3…).

Orbit until an anchor swings behind the building — that leg draws dashed and dimmed (ISO 128: dashed means hidden) while the label stays at full strength.
