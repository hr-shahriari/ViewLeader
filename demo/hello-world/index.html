About this example

The question: what is the least code that puts a leader on a Three.js model?

Try: left-drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. The note re-projects every frame, and rails outside the building’s projected outline instead of landing on top of it, because the adapter was given modelBounds. Nothing here is editable — that starts on Direct editing.

API: new ViewLeader({ boundary, adapters }) · createThreeAdapter({ camera, renderer, modelBounds }) · annotations.create() · leader.update()
