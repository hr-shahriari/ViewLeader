About this example

The question: how do you save a camera framing, and play a tour of them, without editing the annotation document?

Try: orbit anywhere, then Overview or Corner to restore a framing — the real Three.js camera moves, because the host’s viewerState adapter is what writes it. Play tour runs both steps, 700 ms of transition and 400 ms of dwell each. Activation is transactional: the adapter’s rollback puts the camera back if applying fails.

Restore Corner and the leader draws dashed and dimmed: that framing sits low enough to put the front wall between the camera and the roof slab this note anchors (ISO 128: dashed means hidden). Orbit back up and it goes solid again.

API: views.save({ id, name }) · views.activate(id) · views.createTour({ steps }) · views.playTour(id) · adapters.viewerState (capture / prepare / apply / rollback)
