About this example

The question: what can a label carry, and how is its leader line routed to it?

Try: seven notes ring the building — tag, callout, split callout, symbolic block, a right-to-left plain note, a host-resolved image, and Markdown drawn by a plugin. The document never names a URL: the host’s images.resolve turns asset:detail into bytes. Change Leader routing to re-route all seven at once and compare the three modes.

Orbit until an anchor swings behind the building — that leg draws dashed and dimmed (ISO 128: dashed means hidden) while the label stays at full strength.

Occlusion sits beside the routing select because it runs after routing, not instead of it: the mode picks the path, this grades the part of it that turns out to be behind the building. keep, the default, dashes and dims a buried leg. fade also drops a note whose every leg is buried to a quarter opacity — one leg each here, so that is any buried note. hide does not draw a buried leg at all, stroke, arrowhead and click target with it, so the note goes until the camera lets its anchor be seen again.

API: annotations.create() · annotations.reroute(id, { kind: 'automatic', mode }) · annotations.update(id, { occlusion }) · history.transaction() · adapters.images.resolve · plugins: [markdownPlugin]
