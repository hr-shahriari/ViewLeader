About this example

The question: how do the annotations get out of the browser and into Solibri, Navisworks or Revit Issues?

Try: the three notes are exported already — the status line names real ZIP entries and their topic GUIDs, read back out of the archive with readArchive. Clear the document empties it and orbits away, so the notes exist only as bytes; Import BCF reads those same bytes back onto the same elements and restores the camera from the viewpoint stored in the file. Nothing is downloaded — the whole trip is one Uint8Array. It is lossy by design: BCF carries a topic, a comment and a viewpoint, so content kind, style, manual placement, routing, extra leader legs, regions, ink and your own metadata do not make the trip.

Orbit until an anchor swings behind the building — that leg draws dashed and dimmed (ISO 128: dashed means hidden) while the label stays at full strength.

API: interchange.exportBcf · interchange.readArchive · interchange.parseBcf · interchange.planBcfApply · annotations.create · history.transaction
